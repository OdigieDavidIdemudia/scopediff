ScopeDiff
Watches bug bounty scopes and tells hunters exactly what changed, before they waste a weekend.



Problem
Scope changes silently across HackerOne, Bugcrowd and Intigriti. Hunters test removed assets and miss newly added ones.

MVP
One worker for two platforms, versioned scope diff, email/Slack alert, a web feed with filters. No scanning, no exploitation.

Demo
Show a program, simulate a scope change, then show the alert and the exact diff it produced.

Stack
FastAPI
Postgres
React
APScheduler
Slack webhook
